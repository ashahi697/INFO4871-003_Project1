Science Fiction

Science Fiction books are fictional works that explore imaginary concepts such as futuristic science and technology, space exploration, time travel, and extraterrestrial life. Some examples of science fiction books include classic novels like "1984" and "Brave New World", as well as modern series like "The Hunger Games" and "Dune".
